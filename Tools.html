The Writing Toolkit

Writing Tools

Writing Supplies

This may seem obvious, but here's some items that can help you begin writing.

  • A Notebook
  • Writing Utensils (pen, pencil, marker, crayon, color pencil, etc.)
  • Highlighter or Sticky Notes
  • Dictionary
  • Thesaurus
  • Electronic writing device (computer, tablet, phone)
