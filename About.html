About the "Like Totally Writing" Website

"Like Totally Writing" is a writing-focused website designed to help teenagers in the South Jersey area find their voice without relying on generative AI.

There are many websites focused on writing advice and writing practice, and thousands of resources. However, I noticed that many of these are targeting adults, like parents and teachers, instead of the teens it would benefit. So, I decided to make one.

This website includes writing exercises, prompts, tools and resources, author advice, meetup opportunities, schoolarship options, and an entire page explaining why AI use should be limited.

Generative AI is quickly becoming an unavoidable part of our lives, especially in the world of writing. From a Nobel laureate admitting they used AI in their work to Barnes and Noble stating that they are considering stocking AI generated books, it feels like there is a new revelation about AI writing every day. With that said, I'd like to try and counteract the negative impacts caused by generative AI with something positive.
