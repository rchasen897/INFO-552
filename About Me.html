    Rebecca Chasen

    The Author of Like Totally Writing

    Former Teen, Current Adult

    Hi! My name is Rebecca (she/her) and I'm an aspiring librarian in my third year at Drexel. I originally started my career in Communications/Marketing, but pivotted into Library Science and Customer Service a few years ago.

    I fell in love with writing in elementary school, and I've never really looked back. I started with fanfiction, and branched out into short stories. In college, I got a minor in Creative Writing, and branched out into writing news articles, scripts, and more. Currently, I'm working on more long-form writing projects, although I don't expect to complete them any time soon.

    Nowadays, I run my own D&D game, read by pool, go to the Farmers Market, cook fun recipes, play Stardew Valley, get yelled at by my cats, and most of all, I write.
