The Writing Toolkit

Writing Prompts

DON'T OVERTHINK IT

The best way to utilize these prompts is to choose one and start writing.

Prompt 1

A man is sitting at a table on his own in a crowded Italian restaurant. A woman from another table gets up, walks over to his table, and sits down opposite him.

Write three versions of this scene from three different perspectives.

Write the first scene in first person POV from the perspective of one of the characters.

Write the second scene in third person POV.

Write the third scene using just dialogue.

Prompt 2

Write a story in the form of a recipe.

Make sure there's ingredients and intructions, although you can add a preamble before the recipe (like many recipe blogs).

Prompt 3

Go to the thrift store or think about your latest trip. Pick one item you find interesting and write a story about it.

When writing, imagine who the past owner was, how they used the item, and why they donated it.
